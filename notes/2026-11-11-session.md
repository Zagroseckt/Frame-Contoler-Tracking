## Research findings (with sources)

1. Frame controllers: **18 IR LEDs each**, ringless, face + handle + bottom `[P]` (UploadVR,
   VR.org, press hands-ons). Tracked by the headset's 4 monochrome fisheye cameras plus IR
   illuminators, fused with IMU `[D]` (Valve support + specs).
2. Quest 3 Touch Plus: **8 IR LEDs** (7 face, 1 handle), IMU, fused with continuous hand
   tracking `[D]` (Meta, Road to VR, UploadVR).
3. Index controllers are lighthouse *receivers* — they emit nothing `[D]`. Frame reportedly does
   not support outside-in/lighthouse `[P]`. This reshapes the far goal: it must be a custom
   emitter rig, not a pairing.
4. Valve ships **Record Tracking Data** in Developer settings, with optional camera video `[D]`.
   This is the best candidate for a near-raw tracking-camera view.
5. OpenVR device ceiling: `k_unMaxTrackedDeviceCount = 64` `[D]` (`openvr.h`). Vive guidance:
   11 trackers (13 without controllers) inside 16 devices `[D]`. Frame's own ceiling: unpublished `[U]`.
