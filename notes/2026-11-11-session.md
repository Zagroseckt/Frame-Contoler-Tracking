## Research findings (with sources)

1. Frame controllers: **18 IR LEDs each**, ringless, face + handle + bottom `[P]` (UploadVR,
   VR.org, press hands-ons). Tracked by the headset's 4 monochrome fisheye cameras plus IR
   illuminators, fused with IMU `[D]` (Valve support + specs).
2. Quest 3 Touch Plus: **8 IR LEDs** (7 face, 1 handle), IMU, fused with continuous hand
   tracking `[D]` (Meta, Road to VR, UploadVR).
3. Index controllers are lighthouse *receivers* — they emit nothing `[D]`. Frame reportedly does
   not support outside-in/lighthouse `[P]`. This reshapes the far goal: it must be a custom
   emitter rig, not a pairing.
4. Valve ships **Record Tracking Data** in Developer settings, with optional camera video `[D]`.
   This is the best candidate for a near-raw tracking-camera view.
5. OpenVR device ceiling: `k_unMaxTrackedDeviceCount = 64` `[D]` (`openvr.h`). Vive guidance:
   11 trackers (13 without controllers) inside 16 devices `[D]`. Frame's own ceiling: unpublished `[U]`.

## Part 2 — the Frame came online

Headset woke up at ~11:05. Ran `scripts/frame-check.sh`, then went in.

### The stack

Six outward cameras, not four. `XRService` (tracking engine) + `dsp_service` + `vrdashboard` +
`v4l2cam`, with `libArcturusPerception.so` doing perception, ONNX Runtime and MediaPipe for NNs.
Full map in `docs/07-ondevice-map.md`.

### The constellation (biggest find)

Controller models are plain JSON, one per serial, in `~/.config/openvr/config/roy/`:

- `6cb1df7a24e1.json` → `Roy_Right`
- `ebc3df5704f3.json` → `Roy_Left`

**18 model points + 18 normals each**, IMU extrinsics included, left/right mirror images.
Bounding box ≈ 64 × 114 × 66 mm, closest LED pair 12.3 mm, 3 LEDs on the lower handle.

A second variant exists in resources: `Roy_K2V3_Right_CV`, **17 points**. So LED count varies by
revision. Press said 18; correct for retail.

Also found per-serial IMU calibration history (`controllerCalibration/controllerFromImuHistory/`)
with acc/gyro bias, scale, cross-axis and `controllerFromImu` quaternions — real calibration data,
not placeholders.

### Pairing internals

`driver_cv.so` has `CRoyPairing` with log strings for OOBE discovery, bootstrap, timed pairing,
identify, unpair. So pairing is **radio-first**, and the optical model file is a *product* of
pairing. That reframes the Quest 3 plan: without a radio bootstrap, there is no serial to attach
a model to.

### Cameras and illumination

- SLAM pair: `og01a1b`, `/dev/video9` + `/dev/video13`, 1056×1024 GREY
- Upper pair: `og0ve10`, `/dev/video6` + `/dev/video7`, 640×480 GREY
- Passthrough pair: `arcimx616`, `/dev/video0` + `/dev/video3`, 2464×2464 NV12
- All tracking nodes are exclusively held by XRService (`EBUSY` on stream request)
- IR illuminator at `/sys/class/leds/mp3317-scene-illum-ir/`, brightness 0–4095, mode `Auto`

### The settings goldmine

`XRServiceSettings.json` currently holds one key (`lightSourceFrequencyEstimate: 60`), but
`XRService` recognises a large schema (`captureSessionSettings/trackingCameras/...`,
`minControllerSettings`, etc.) and 9,150 candidate option names. Relevant ones:

`enableFewLedTrackingMode`, `estimateYawFor1LEDControllers`,
`filterTrackedLedsForOtherControllers`, `alternateNNTrackingBetweenControllers`,
`fallbackToPoseResidualIfNotAllLedsAreVisbled`, `disableLedBootstrapping`, `debugVis`,
`displayOverlay`, `detectMarkers`.

This is the first real "turn a knob" lever we've had. Untested until we're willing to restart
SteamVR.

### Tooling that works today

`trackingtest` (needs `LD_LIBRARY_PATH=.`) streams live HMD pose. `vrcmd --info` lists devices.
`vrcmd --pollcontrollers` blocks waiting for controller events.
